## Engineering interpretation

Use white/light-gray surfaces, inexpensive opaque blue-gray glass, shallow
aluminum divisions, canopy structures and rectangular roofs. Recessed window
appearance comes from panels behind projecting facade frames. Use tropical
broadleaf silhouettes, clipped shrubs, lawns, paved paths and a generic rolling
green backdrop. Plant species are deliberately unspecified.

All positions, footprints, road geometry, camera positions, tree sizes and hill
shapes are invented in a local Cartesian frame. The 20 m office, 25 m laboratory
and 30 m workshop heights are user-supplied synthetic estimates, not measured
PTIC heights. Lighting presets are artistic generic daylight conditions. They
have no relationship to real-site solar angles, weather, flight routes or launch
positions. Materials are approximations, not measured optical properties.

Source images were downloaded for inspection only into the timestamped Phase 1C
evidence directory under `logs/ptic_environment/`; actual simulation screenshots
are stored separately under `screenshots/ptic_environment/`.
